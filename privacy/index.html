Privacy, without surprises.

PacePOP is offline-first. Here is what the current iPhone release handles and what remains on your device.

Information PacePOP handles

PacePOP stores the tasks, projects, milestones, journal entries, check-ins, preferences, and manual fitness records you create. If you choose to connect Apple Fitness or Apple Calendar, PacePOP can also store a local copy of selected workout summaries or calendar event details.

Apple Fitness and HealthKit

Apple Fitness imports include workout type, start time, and duration. PacePOP reads this information through HealthKit only after you choose Import and grant permission. PacePOP does not write to HealthKit.

Apple Calendar

Calendar imports include event title, start time, and duration. Import is one-way; PacePOP does not edit or delete calendar events.

Storage and transmission

The current release stores PacePOP content locally on your iPhone. It does not provide an account or cloud sync and does not transmit your content to PacePOP-operated servers. PacePOP does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Notifications

Local notifications may be scheduled for reminders you choose. Apple manages notification delivery and HealthKit and Calendar permissions through iOS.

Deleting your data

You can delete PacePOP content and scheduled PacePOP reminders from Me → Delete all PacePOP data. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored app data. Removing data from PacePOP does not delete source records held by Apple Fitness, Health, or Calendar.

Children

PacePOP is a general productivity app and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when PacePOP’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request. Do not include health information, calendar details, or other sensitive personal data in a public request.
