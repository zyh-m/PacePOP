Support

How can we help?

A few quick answers for planning, connected apps, reminders, and your locally stored data.

Where is my PacePOP information stored?

PacePOP 3.1 stores its content locally on your iPhone. This release has no PacePOP account or cloud sync.

How do I import Apple Fitness workouts?

Open Me → Connected apps → Apple Fitness → Import, then allow workout read access. PacePOP imports workout type, start time, and duration through HealthKit; it never writes health data.

How does Calendar import work?

Open Me → Connected apps → Apple Calendar → Import. PacePOP makes a one-way local copy of upcoming event titles, times, and durations. It never edits your Apple Calendar events.

How do I remove my data?

Open Me → Delete all PacePOP data. This removes tasks, projects, journal entries, check-ins, imported copies, fitness records, and scheduled PacePOP reminders from the iPhone.

Still need help?

Open a support request on GitHub. Please describe what happened and your iOS version, but do not include health information, calendar details, passwords, or other sensitive data.
